DOUBLECUP's PNG Payload, (Mon, Aug 24th)

Threat: Ransomware
Severity: 6/10

The script begins with a Windows carriage‑return/line‑feed (0x0D 0x0A), allowing attackers to extract it easily using the built‑in FINDSTR utility rather than a custom decoder. This simple trick undermines the purported stealth of the infection.
