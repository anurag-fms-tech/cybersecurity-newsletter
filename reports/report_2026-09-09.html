Stealing AI Reasoning Traces

Threat: Vulnerability
Severity: 7/10

Researchers discovered a flaw in major LLM APIs that lets encrypted “chain‑of‑thought” blocks be reused across sessions, users and models. By injecting a trace from a strong model into a weaker one, attackers can force the weaker model to decrypt and reveal the original reasoning in plaintext. The exploit enables IP theft, large‑scale PII and credential leaks, exposure of hidden harmful content, and invisible prompt‑injection attacks. The authors disclosed the issue responsibly and suggest cryptographic and system‑level fixes.
