New DDRop Attack Breaks Intel TDX and AMD SEV-SNP Confidential Computing

Threat: Zero-Day
Severity: 9/10

Researchers unveiled DDRop, a $200 interposer that silently drops writes on DDR5 memory, letting Intel TDX, Scalable SGX and AMD SEV‑SNP processors read stale encrypted data as current. By exploiting the lack of “freshness” guarantees, the attack—requiring brief physical access to insert the board—breaks integrity of confidential‑computing workloads in cloud servers. The team will release designs and code on GitHub.
