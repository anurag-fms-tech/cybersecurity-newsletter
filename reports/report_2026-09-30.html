French Tax Data Theft Using Stolen Staff Passwords Went Undetected for Seven Weeks

Threat: Data Breach
Severity: 8/10

In June‑July, attackers stole staff passwords at France’s tax administration (DGFIP) and accessed the E‑Contact messaging tool, extracting data on ~350 000 individuals and ~250 000 businesses. Weak login protection, poorly segmented networks and monitoring gaps enabled the breach, though no data left the agency’s servers. New Spectre-v2 BTR Attack Leaks Linux Memory Despite Existing Defenses

Threat: Vulnerability
Severity: 9/10

Researchers from VUSec and Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna have identified a new Spectre v2 variant, dubbed Branch Target Reuse (BTR), that exploits stale indirect‑branch predictions in JIT engines and the Linux kernel. BTR affects browsers, language runtimes and kernel JITs across CPUs, enabling transient execute‑after‑free attacks. Proof‑of‑concept exploits can steal a root password hash on fully patched Intel systems within minutes.

Severity: 9/10
