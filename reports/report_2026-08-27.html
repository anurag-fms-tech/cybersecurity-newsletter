New GPUThor Rowhammer Defeats ECC on NVIDIA RTX A6000 to Gain Host Root Access

Threat: Vulnerability
Severity: 9/10

Researchers at the University of Toronto unveiled “GPUThor,” a Rowhammer attack that flips bits in NVIDIA Ampere‑class workstation GPUs with GDDR6 memory, bypassing ECC and the vendor’s Rowhammer mitigation. By running an unprivileged CUDA kernel, attackers can cause denial‑of‑service or gain a root shell via non‑uniform hammering that evades TRR. NVIDIA advises enabling system‑level ECC, avoiding cross‑tenant GPU sharing, monitoring ECC counters and restricting untrusted CUDA workloads.

Who Has Admin Rights in your Entra ID Directory?, (Wed, Aug 26th)

Threat: Vulnerability
Severity: 2/10

A key security risk in Entra ID (and any platform) is unmanaged admin privileges. Organizations often grant too many users—sometimes former employees or non‑technical staff—rights that let them delete or alter critical settings. Auditors focus on “excess admins,” a control highlighted in CIS Critical Controls. Using PowerShell (Connect‑MgGraph, Get‑MgDirectoryRole/Member) you can enumerate role counts, then list each user’s assignments to verify that only appropriate accounts retain Global Admin, Intune, or other high‑privilege roles. Regular reviews prevent accidental or malicious damage.
