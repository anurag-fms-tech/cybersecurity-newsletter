Who Has Admin Rights in your Entra ID Directory?, (Wed, Aug 26th)

Threat: Vulnerability
Severity: 3/10

A key cybersecurity risk in Entra (and any identity platform) is uncontrolled admin privileges—who can delete, modify, or configure critical resources. Organizations often have too many admins, granting entry‑level staff excessive rights, which auditors flag under CIS Critical Controls (v7 #4, v8 #6). PowerShell scripts can audit Entra ID, revealing role counts and specific users, helping spot stale or unnecessary Global Administrators, Readers, and other privileged accounts before they cause accidental or malicious damage.
