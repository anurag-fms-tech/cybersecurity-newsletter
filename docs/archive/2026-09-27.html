One URL, Three Different Tricks, (Thu, Sep 24th)

Threat: Malware
Severity: 8/10

A phishing email used a specially crafted URL that exploits parser differences. It inserts a random “user:password@” segment before the “@” to defeat exact‑match blocklists and appear as an email address. The hostname “gynd--.koncar-hr.com” starts/ends with hyphens—invalid for strict validators but accepted by browsers and DNS, enabling wildcard, unique subdomains. The victim’s email is appended in the path, allowing the page to pre‑fill login fields and trick parsers into seeing the attacker’s domain as a trusted one. Detection: look for URLs with multiple “@”, hyphen‑bordered labels, and paths containing the recipient’s email.
