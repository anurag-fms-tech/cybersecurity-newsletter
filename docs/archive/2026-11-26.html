A brief history of syslog timestamps, (Sun, Nov 22nd)

Threat: Vulnerability
Severity: 1/10

Debian 12 (Bookworm) and Ubuntu 23.04 stopped installing rsyslog by default and removed the RSYSLOG_TraditionalFileFormat directive, which had forced the historic “Mmm dd hh:mm:ss” BSD timestamp. As a result, rsyslog now emits RFC 3339/ISO‑8601 timestamps, matching the newer syslog standard (RFC 5424). Fedora, AlmaLinux and other Red Hat‑based distros still keep the directive, so their logs retain the older format.
