Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

A Group‑IB investigation uncovered the “0ktapus” phishing campaign that spoofed Okta’s MFA login page, compromising 9,931 accounts across more than 130 organizations—including Twilio and Cloudflare. Attackers first hit telecom firms to harvest phone numbers, then sent SMS links to fake Okta pages, stealing credentials and MFA codes. The breach affected 114 U.S. firms and victims in 68 other countries, and its full scope remains unknown.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

LockBit remains the dominant ransomware gang, delivering 62 attacks in July—double the total of the next two groups. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged, up 440 % and 50 % since June, after Conti’s split and U.S. bounty on its members. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47 % in July, hinting at further growth as Conti’s offshoots gain footholds.
