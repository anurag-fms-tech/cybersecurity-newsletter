Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6 (Reason: Ransomware attacks are common and can be damaging, but the specific impact and details are not provided. This vague report does not indicate the severity of the current situation, making a moderate score more accurate.)/10

Lockbit is the most prolific ransomware group after a summer resurgence in attacks, followed by Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. There were 198 successful ransomware campaigns in July, a 47% increase from June. The rise of these groups is linked to Conti, which restructured and split into associates and replacements, including Hiveleaks and BlackBasta.
