Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Ransomware attacks are back on the rise after a recent dip, led by old groups like Lockbit, followed closely by Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. Lockbit accounted for 62 attacks in July, a significant increase from the previous month. Hiveleaks saw a 440% rise in attacks since June, while BlackBasta rose 50%.
