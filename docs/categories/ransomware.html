Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Ransomware attacks are back on the rise, with Lockbit leading 62 attacks in July, followed by Hiveleaks and BlackBasta with 27 and 24 attacks respectively. These groups are associated with the disbanded Conti group and have seen significant increases in activity, with Hiveleaks rising 440% since June.
