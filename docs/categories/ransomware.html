Canadian Man Pleads Guilty in Snowflake Extortions

Severity: 8/10

A 26‑year‑old Canadian hacker, Connor Riley Moucka (“Judische”/“Waifu”), pleaded guilty to computer fraud and a conspiracy that stole and extorted data from more than 165 Snowflake‑hosted customers, including Ticketmaster, Lending Tree and AT&T. He also pilfered call/text records of 100 million AT&T users and other personal data, extorting victims for over $2.5 million before his October 2024 arrest.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

A Group‑IB report links a massive “0ktapus” phishing campaign to the compromise of 9,931 accounts across more than 130 organizations, including Twilio and Cloudflare. Attackers spoofed Okta’s multi‑factor authentication pages via SMS links, harvesting both credentials and MFA codes. They initially targeted telecom firms to collect phone numbers, then broadened to 114 U.S. companies and 68 other countries. The full scale remains unknown.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

LockBit dominated July’s ransomware landscape with 62 attacks, outpacing the next two groups—Hiveleaks (27) and BlackBasta (24)—combined. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47% from June, though still below the spring peak of ~300. Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, both offshoots of the disrupted Conti gang, surged after U.S. pressure on Conti, signaling a resurgence of old RaaS actors and likely further growth into August.
