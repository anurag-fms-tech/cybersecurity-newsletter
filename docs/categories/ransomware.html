Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks have increased, with Lockbit being the most prolific group in July, behind 62 attacks, followed by Hiveleaks with 27 and BlackBasta with 24. Successful ransomware campaigns rose 47% in July, with nearly 200 recorded. The resurgence may be linked to the restructuring of Conti, a former top ransomware group, following a US government crackdown offering up to $15 million for information.
