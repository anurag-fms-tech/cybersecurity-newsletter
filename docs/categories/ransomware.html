Shai-Hulud Attack Nips Cyber-Firm CrowdSec's GitHub Data

Severity: 6/10

Former CrowdSec employee’s machine was infected by the Shai‑Hulud worm, exposing an OAuth token that granted read access to the firm’s private GitHub repos. Attackers used the token to download roughly 170 private repositories within minutes on May 22, 2026. The breach was discovered on Sept 16 after the code surfaced on a cyber‑crime forum. CrowdSec confirmed only source code was taken; infrastructure and databases remain intact.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 7/10

A massive phishing operation dubbed “0ktapus” compromised 9,931 accounts at more than 130 organizations—including Twilio, Cloudflare, and 114 U.S. firms—by spoofing Okta’s multi‑factor authentication (MFA) portal. Attackers first targeted telecom providers to harvest phone numbers, then sent SMS links to fake Okta login pages, stealing credentials and MFA codes. The full scope remains unknown.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

LockBit dominated July’s ransomware scene, delivering 62 attacks—double the combined total of the next two groups. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged, up 440% and 50% since June, after restructuring from the Conti gang following a U.S. bounty on Conti members. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47% to 198 in July, hinting at continued growth into August.
