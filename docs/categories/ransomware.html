Canadian Man Pleads Guilty in Snowflake Extortions

Severity: 6/10

Connor Riley Moucka, a 26‑year‑old Canadian dubbed “Judische”/“Waifu,” pleaded guilty to computer fraud and a conspiracy that stole cloud data from at least 165 Snowflake customers and call‑text records of over 100 million AT&T users. Using stolen credentials lacking MFA, he extorted firms such as Ticketmaster and Neiman Marcus, netting $2.5 million. Snowflake tightened password and MFA rules, and Moucka also threatened officials and co‑conspirator U.S. Army soldier Cameron Wagenius. (≈60 words)

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9/10

Group‑IB researchers identified the “0ktapus” campaign, an operation that spoofed Okta MFA pages and compromised 9,931 accounts at more than 130 companies, including Twilio and Cloudflare. Attackers first hit telecom firms to harvest phone numbers, then sent SMS links to employees, stealing Okta credentials and MFA codes. Impacts span 114 US firms and 68 other nations; the scope remains unclear.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 7/10

LockBit dominated July’s ransomware landscape with 62 attacks, outpacing rivals Hiveleaks (27) and BlackBasta (24). Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47 % to 198 incidents, though still below the spring peak of ~300. Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, both offshoots of the dismantled Conti gang, have surged (440 % and 50 % since June). Analysts warn LockBit 3.0 remains the most threatening group, and the Conti split may fuel further growth.
