Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Lockbit is the most prolific ransomware group this summer, behind 62 attacks in July, exceeding the combined count of its competitors, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. Reconstructing an Akira Ransomware Kill Chain from Perimeter and Endpoint Logs, (Wed, May 27th)

Severity: 8/10

A cybersecurity expert details a recent Akira-Attributed intrusion at a mid-sized organization, reconstructing the attack using only SSLVPN syslog and Windows event log exports. They identified a brute-force attack on a local SSLVPN account, which had been disabled in Active Directory but remained provisioned, and used credential stuffing to gain domain admin access.

Silent Ransom Group Uses In-Person IT Impersonation to Breach Systems

Severity: 8/10

Law firms in the US are being targeted by the Silent Ransom Group (SRG), who are using increasingly sophisticated tactics. The group has evolved from phishing emails to impersonating trusted IT staff in phone calls and face-to-face encounters, gaining access to corporate systems and extorting victims. This campaign has been observed since 2023 and has affected companies in multiple sectors.
