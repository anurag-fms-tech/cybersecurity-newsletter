Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

A massive phishing campaign dubbed “0ktapus” compromised 9,931 accounts across more than 130 organizations, including Twilio and Cloudflare. Attackers spoofed Okta’s MFA login page via SMS links, stealing identity credentials and MFA codes. They initially targeted telecom firms to harvest phone numbers, then sent fake Okta links worldwide—114 U.S. firms and 68 other countries. The full scale remains unclear.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 9/10

Lockbit remains the most active ransomware gang, delivering 62 attacks in July—double the total of the next two groups. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged, up 440 % and 50 % since June, both offshoots of the disrupted Conti operation. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47 % in July, though still below the spring peak. Experts warn the Conti split may fuel further growth.
