Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Ransomware attacks are back on the rise with Lockbit as the most prolific group, behind 62 attacks in July, followed by Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. According to NCC Group data, successful ransomware campaigns rose 47% in July to 198, with a 440% and 50% rise for Hiveleaks and BlackBasta respectively since June.
