Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Ransomware attacks are surging, led by Lockbit, with 62 attacks in July, followed by Hiveleaks (27) and BlackBasta (24). This resurgence is connected to the restructuring of Conti, which the US government previously targeted. Lockbit's lead has been called 'the most threatening ransomware group', and researchers expect further increases in attacks from Hiveleaks and BlackBasta in August.
