Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks are back on the rise, led by old groups. Data from NCC Group shows Lockbit as the most prolific ransomware gang in July, behind 62 attacks. Two of its offshoots, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, have seen rapid rises in attacks, 440% and 50% respectively since June. The resurgence may be connected to the US government's efforts against Russian cybercrime in May.
