Japan Announces Emergency Cybersecurity Package Following a Wave of Attacks

Severity: 8/10

Japan announced a ¥10 billion emergency cybersecurity package after a wave of data breaches hit dozens of firms and government agencies. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi stressed rapid, AI‑driven response and “active cyber defense.” The plan creates a Ministry‑led “top‑gun” AI team, a public‑private “cyber‑rescue” unit, and a national support team from defense, police and cybersecurity agencies to share intel, protect vulnerable firms and boost Japan’s overall cyber resilience.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

A massive phishing operation, dubbed “0ktapus,” compromised 9,931 accounts across more than 130 organizations, including Twilio and Cloudflare. Attackers spoofed Okta’s multi‑factor authentication pages, sending SMS links that harvested users’ Okta credentials and MFA codes. The campaign, traced to Group‑IB, began by targeting telecom firms to collect phone numbers, then expanded globally to 114 U.S. companies and 68 other countries. The full scope remains unknown.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Lockbit dominated July’s ransomware landscape with 62 attacks—double the next two groups combined. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged 440% and 50% since June, respectively. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47% to 198, though still below the spring peak of ~300. Researchers link the rebound to Conti’s fragmentation: Hiveleaks is an affiliate, BlackBasta a replacement strain, emerging after a U.S. bounty spurred structural changes in the original gang.
