Ransomware attacks are back on the rise, led by old groups. Lockbit is the most prolific ransomware gang, trailing two offshoots of the Conti group, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. There were 198 successful ransomware campaigns in July, a 47% increase from June, and the groups' rises are connected to Conti's restructuring.
