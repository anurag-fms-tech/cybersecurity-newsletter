Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Lockbit was the most prolific ransomware group in July, behind 62 attacks, according to data from NCC Group. The group's resurgence is part of a broader increase in ransomware attacks, with 198 successful campaigns in July, a 47% rise from June. Lockbit's dominance is linked to the decline of Conti, with offshoots Hiveleaks and BlackBasta also experiencing rapid growth, associated with Conti's restructuring.
