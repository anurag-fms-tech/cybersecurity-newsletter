Silent Ransom Group Hits US Law Firms in Escalating Extortion Attacks

Severity: 8/10

A financially motivated threat group, UNC3753, is targeting US legal and financial services firms with a data theft extortion campaign, using vishing, IT impersonation, and in-person office intrusions to steal data. The group uses phishing emails and voice calls to gain access, then exfiltrates data and demands ransoms, often in under a day, with some incidents moving from compromise to data exfiltration in under an hour.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Lockbit is the most prolific ransomware group this summer, behind 62 attacks in July, more than twice as many as the second and third most groups combined. Other groups, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, have also seen significant rises in attacks, with a 440% and 50% increase respectively. Total ransomware campaigns surged 47% in July, possibly due to Conti's restructuring, with Hiveleaks and BlackBasta being its offshoots.
