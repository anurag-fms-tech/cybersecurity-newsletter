Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks are on the rise, led by the Lockbit group, which carried out 62 attacks in July. Two offshoots of the Conti group, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, saw significant increases, with 440% and 50% rises respectively. Overall, 198 successful ransomware campaigns occurred in July, a 47% increase from June. This resurgence may be connected to the restructuring of Conti, now appearing under new identities.
