Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks are back on the rise with 198 successful campaigns in July, a 47% increase from June. Lockbit led the way with 62 attacks, followed by Hiveleaks (27) and BlackBasta (24). These two groups are offshoots of the Conti ransomware gang, which split into two groups after the US government offered a $15 million reward for information leading to the downfall of Conti.
