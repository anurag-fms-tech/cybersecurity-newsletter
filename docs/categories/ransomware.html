Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 9/10

Ransomware attacks have surged back, with Lockbit as the most prolific group in July, behind 62 attacks, followed by Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks). Lockbit's resurgence and the rise of these two new groups are likely connected to Conti's restructuring, with BlackBasta replacing Conti as a replacement strain and Hiveleaks as an affiliate. Total ransomware campaigns increased 47% in July to 198.
