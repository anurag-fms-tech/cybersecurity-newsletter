Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 7/10

A Group‑IB investigation links a massive phishing operation—dubbed “0ktapus”—to the theft of Okta identity credentials and MFA codes from 9,931 accounts across more than 130 organizations, including Twilio and Cloudflare. Attackers first compromised telecom firms to harvest phone numbers, then sent SMS links to fake Okta login pages. The campaign affected 114 U.S. companies and victims in 68 other countries; its full scope remains unclear.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

LockBit remains the dominant ransomware group, delivering 62 attacks in July—double the total of the next two contenders. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged, up 440% and 50% since June, after the Conti gang’s split and U.S. bounty on its members. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47% in July, though still below the spring peak of ~300 incidents.
