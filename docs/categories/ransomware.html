Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Lockbit is the most prolific ransomware group, behind 62 attacks in July, with old groups like Lockbit, Hiveleaks, and BlackBasta driving a resurgence in ransomware attacks. Successful ransomware campaigns rose 47% in July, but still trail the nearly 300 campaigns in March and April. BlackBasta and Hiveleaks, associated with Conti, saw sharp rises of 440% and 50% respectively.
