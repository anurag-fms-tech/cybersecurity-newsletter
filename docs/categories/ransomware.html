Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Lockbit ransomware group led 62 attacks in July, the most of the summer, surpassing previous months and twice that of the second and third most prolific groups, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. Ransomware attacks overall rose 47% from June, with 198 successful campaigns, and the resurgence may be connected to a rebranding of former Conti group, with new affiliates such as Hiveleaks and BlackBasta.

Ransomware gangs find Europe’s weakest link in third-party suppliers

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks against European organizations increased by 55.1% between January and April 2026, compared to the same period in 2025. Manufacturing was the hardest-hit sector, accounting for 27.9% of incidents, while third-party suppliers became a major entry point for attackers, with Germany recording the highest number of incidents and the UK, France, Italy, and Spain following close behind.
