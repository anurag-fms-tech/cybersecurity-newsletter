Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

NCC Group data shows a resurgence in ransomware attacks, led by old groups, with Lockbit as the most prolific in July behind 62 attacks, 10 more than the previous month and more than twice as many as the next two groups combined, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta. The figures represent a 440% rise for Hiveleaks and 50% rise for BlackBasta since June, likely connected to Conti's restructuring.
