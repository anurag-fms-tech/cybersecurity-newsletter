Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Lockbit is the most prolific ransomware group, accounting for 62 attacks in July, according to NCC Group data. Its popularity is followed by Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, which experienced significant growth after previously belonging to the Conti group. The total number of successful ransomware campaigns in July rose 47% from June, and researchers speculate that Conti's restructuring has led to its presence resurfacing.
