Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6/10

Lockbit ransomware group is leading the resurgence of ransomware attacks, behind 62 attacks in July. Other notable groups include Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks), which are associated with Conti, a previously top ransomware gang. According to NCC Group data, there were 198 successful ransomware campaigns in July, a 47% increase from June, but still lower than the spring peak.
