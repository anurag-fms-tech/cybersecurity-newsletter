Two Alleged ‘TeamPCP’ Hackers Arrested in Australia

Severity: 4/10

Australian police arrested two Western Australian men, 21 and 23, alleged members of the TeamPCP cyber‑crime syndicate that has run the longest‑standing software‑supply‑chain campaign. TeamPCP injected malicious code into popular open‑source tools, used a self‑propagating worm (Shai‑Hulud) to steal developer credentials, ran a $1,000 Monero contest to recruit attackers, and recently hijacked the LiteLLM AI gateway, compromising secrets from over 2,500 organizations.

Canadian Man Pleads Guilty in Snowflake Extortions

Severity: 8/10

A 26‑year‑old Canadian, Connor Riley Moucka (aka “Judische”/“Waifu”), pleaded guilty to computer fraud and a conspiracy that hacked and extorted over 165 Snowflake‑cloud customers, including Ticketmaster and Neiman Marcus, and stole call/text records of 100 million AT&T users. He and co‑conspirators stole billions of personal records, demanded $2.5 million in ransoms, and re‑extorted a government official’s family. Snowflake now mandates stronger passwords and MFA.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9/10

A massive phishing operation, dubbed “0ktapus,” compromised 9,931 accounts across more than 130 organizations—including Twilio, Cloudflare, and 114 US firms—by spoofing Okta’s multi‑factor authentication (MFA) system. Attackers sent SMS links to fake Okta login pages, harvesting credentials and MFA codes. Researchers believe they began by targeting telecoms to collect phone numbers, then expanded globally, with the full impact still unknown.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

LockBit dominated July’s ransomware landscape, delivering 62 attacks—more than double the next two groups combined. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged, up 440 % and 50 % since June, both offshoots of the dismantled Conti gang. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47 % to 198 in July, hinting at a broader resurgence as Conti’s remnants re‑emerge under new names.
