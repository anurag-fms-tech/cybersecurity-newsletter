Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 6 (Ransomware attacks can be serious and have significant consequences, but without more context, it's hard to give a precise ranking.)/10

Ransomware attacks are on the rise, led by old groups such as Lockbit, which was the most prolific in July with 62 attacks. Other groups, Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, also showed significant growth, linked to the restructured Conti gang. Successful ransomware campaigns increased 47% in July, with US government efforts against Russian cybercrime possibly prompting a resurgence in threats.
