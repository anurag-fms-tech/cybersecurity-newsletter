CISA Calls for More Guidance, Less Spin, as Cyber Outages Escalate

Severity: 7/10

A new joint advisory from CISA, the FBI and international partners urges service providers to adopt transparent, immediate breach notification and incident‑response communication. The “Communicating Under Pressure” guide stresses actionable guidance, honest updates and compliance with state and federal reporting laws. As outages and attacks become inevitable, firms grapple with broader reputational, financial and legal operational risks.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

Over 130 companies were hit in a massive phishing campaign that spoofed Okta’s multi‑factor authentication, compromising 9,931 accounts—including Twilio and Cloudflare staff. Named “0ktapus,” the attackers sent SMS links to fake Okta login pages, harvesting credentials and MFA codes. They first targeted telecoms to collect phone numbers, affecting 114 US firms and 68 other countries; the full scope remains unclear.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

LockBit dominated July’s ransomware scene with 62 attacks—double the combined total of the next two groups. Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks) surged, up 440 % and 50 % since June, after the Conti gang fractured under U.S. pressure. Overall ransomware campaigns rose 47 % in July, though still below the spring peak of nearly 300 incidents. Organizations are urged to remain vigilant, especially against LockBit 3.0.
