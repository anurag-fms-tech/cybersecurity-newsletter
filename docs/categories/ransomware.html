Who Runs the Ransomware Group ‘The Gentlemen?’

Severity: 5 (This news appears to be about an investigation or expose on a ransomware group, but it suggests the group is being uncovered or exposed, and may be losing their 'anonymous' status, which could make their attacks more predictable.)/10

The Gentleman ransomware group has risen to the second most active ransomware gang by victim count, claiming over 332 victims since its inception in mid-2025. With a 90/10 affiliate revenue split, the group attracts experienced hackers with its generous payout. Check Point believes the administrator, known as Hastalamuerte or Zeta88, is a Russian-speaking individual with a history of cybercrime forum involvement, primarily based in Izhevsk, Russia.

Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 7/10

This summer, Lockbit has been the most prolific ransomware group, behind 62 attacks in July, with Hiveleaks and BlackBasta following closely. These groups are offshoots of the Conti group, which declined following a US government crackdown. Ransomware attacks are on the rise, with 198 campaigns in July, a 47% increase from June.
