Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks are back on the rise, led by old groups, according to NCC Group data. Lockbit is the most prolific, behind 62 attacks in July, followed by Hiveleaks (27 attacks) and BlackBasta (24 attacks). Successful ransomware campaigns rose 47% in July to 198, with some speculating that the resurgence is related to the restructuring of the Conti group.
