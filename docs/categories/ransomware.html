Ransomware Attacks are on the Rise

Severity: 8/10

Ransomware attacks have resumed their rise, led by old groups like Lockbit, which was behind 62 attacks in July, followed by Hiveleaks (27) and BlackBasta (24). The resurgence may be linked to the restructuring of Conti, as Hiveleaks and BlackBasta, two offshoots, have experienced rapid growth, with 440% and 50% increases, respectively.
