Why Ask Credentials If There Are Secret Codes?, (Wed, Jul 1st)

Severity: 8/10

A phishing campaign targeting Metamask users has been discovered, using pressure tactics to obtain users' secret security phrases. The attackers claim the wallet is at risk and ask users to provide the phrase, exploiting Metamask's password recovery process. The campaign uses a newly registered domain "captcha.solve.help" and is similar to a previous campaign in February.
