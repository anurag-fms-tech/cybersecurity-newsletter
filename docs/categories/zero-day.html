Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9/10

Researchers discovered a massive phishing campaign, dubbed "0ktapus," targeting 130 organizations, including 114 US-based firms and 68 international companies. Attackers sent text messages mimicking Okta's authentication page to obtain user identity credentials and multi-factor authentication (MFA) codes, with 9,931 accounts compromised across various industries. The campaign's true scope remains unknown, but it's believed to have started by targeting telecommunications companies.
