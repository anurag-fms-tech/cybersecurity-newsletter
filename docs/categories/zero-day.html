ShinyHunters Uses Oracle Zero-Day to Rampage Higher Ed

Severity: 8/10

A zero-day vulnerability in Oracle's PeopleSoft software allowed hackers from the ShinyHunters extortion gang to steal data from over 100 organizations, including American universities, exploiting a weakness in the Environment Management Hub (EMHub). The hackers compromised more than 300 PeopleSoft instances with a 9.8 CVSS score vulnerability, CVE-2026-35273.
