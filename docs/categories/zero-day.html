Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8 (Note: This rating may vary based on the context and further information about the specific attack.)/10

A massive phishing campaign known as 0ktapus, targeting 130 organizations globally, compromised 9,931 accounts. The attackers spoofed a multi-factor authentication system to obtain Okta identity credentials and MFA codes. Impacted were 114 US-based firms and companies across 68 other countries. Researchers believe the attackers initially targeted telecommunications companies to obtain phone numbers before sending phishing links via text messages.
