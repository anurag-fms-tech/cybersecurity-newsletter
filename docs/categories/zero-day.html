Offensive Security Investments Surge as AI Threats Increase

Severity: 7/10

Agentic AI has outpaced defenders, prompting firms to boost offensive‑security budgets for penetration testing, vulnerability assessments and red‑team exercises. Omdia analyst Theresa Lanowitz told Dark Reading at Black Hat USA 2026 that enterprises are spending more to match the rapid, AI‑driven weaponisation of new flaws. She adds defenders can also wield AI, but must tightly constrain its capabilities to avoid rogue‑agent fallout. (≈60 words)
