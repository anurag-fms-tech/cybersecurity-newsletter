AI Browsers Vulnerable to 'PleaseFix' Zero-Click Agent Hijacking

Severity: 9/10

Attackers can hijack AI browser agents with zero-click exploits through malicious content. Vulnerable browsers include Claude in Chrome, Gemini, Perplexity Comet, ChatGPT Atlas, and Copilot Edge. The "PleaseFix" threat stems from AI agents failing to reliably distinguish between trusted and untrusted content, allowing attackers to access sensitive data and services. Researchers demonstrated various attack scenarios, including email exfiltration, account takeover, and sensitive data theft.
