GPT-6 Astra Breaks an Old Enigma Message

Severity: 1/10

AI model GPT‑6 Astra cracked an Enigma ciphertext (message 172, “MVUEH”) from Crypto Cellar Research. After scanning the archive, it linked the message to a related text (message 173, “SIPVX”), chose the repeated crib “ROSENOW ROSENOW”, wrote Python and C++ Enigma simulator and Bombe code, and derived the correct key and plaintext. Analysts are reviewing the logs, noting referenced files are not available.

ShinyHunters Claims FBI Hack Via PeopleSoft Zero Day

Severity: 9/10

ShinyHunters claims it breached the FBI using a zero‑day Oracle PeopleSoft flaw, pivoted to AWS GovCloud, and exfiltrated 2‑3 TB of data—including PII for roughly 5,000 employees and applicants. The group posted the leak on its site, alleging the FBI’s May 15 PSA mischaracterized its activities and demanding the notice be removed or revised. It also defaced the FBI jobs portal on Sept 22.
