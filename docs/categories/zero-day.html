Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

A massive phishing campaign, dubbed 0ktapus, compromised 9,931 accounts across 130 organizations in 82 countries, including 114 US-based firms. The primary goal was to gather Okta identity credentials and multi-factor authentication codes, with threat actors using spoofed text messages to mimic Okta authentication pages and trick employees into divulging sensitive information.
