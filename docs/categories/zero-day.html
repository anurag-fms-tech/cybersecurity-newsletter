Kiteworks & Citrix Incidents Show Challenges of Zero-Day Response

Severity: 8/10

GreyNoise flagged a US IP scanning Citrix NetScaler for RCE on Sept. 24, sparking rumors of zero‑day exploits. By Sept. 26, watchTowr urged users to shut down NetScaler, and Citrix released a patch for eight flaws—including two exploited zero‑days—without recommending offline time. In contrast, Kiteworks told customers to power down its data‑protection platform for nine hours before issuing a patch, citing an imminent zero‑day that would affect only ~1 % of users.
