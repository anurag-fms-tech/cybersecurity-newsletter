Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9/10

Over 130 companies have been compromised in a massive phishing campaign that spoofed Okta's multi-factor authentication system. Threat actors, dubbed "0ktapus," targeted employees at organizations such as Twilio and Cloudflare to obtain Okta identity credentials and MFA codes, compromising 9,931 accounts across 182 companies worldwide, with many impacted in the US and other countries.
