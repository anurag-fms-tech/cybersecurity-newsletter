Russian Hackers Exploit New ‘Zero-Click’ Attack Against Western Organizations

Severity: 9/10

Russian state-supported hackers are targeting Western organizations using a Zero-Click method to compromise networks and gain persistent access. The attack exploits a vulnerability in Zimbra Collaboration Suite (ZCS) software, stealing emails and sensitive data by exploiting a view-based exploit via malicious emails. Western cyber intelligence agencies warn that various sectors, including government, defense, and technology, have been targeted since July 2025.
