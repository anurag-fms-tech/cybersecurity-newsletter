ShinyHunters Uses Oracle Zero-Day to Rampage Higher Ed

Severity: 8/10

A zero-day bug in Oracle's PeopleSoft ERP software allowed hackers from the ShinyHunters group to steal data from over 100 organizations, primarily American universities, by exploiting a vulnerability in the Environment Management Hub (EMHub). The bug, assigned CVE-2026-35273, was patched by Oracle after researchers alerted them and the vulnerability was used for remote code execution without authentication from May 27 to June 9, 2026.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9/10

Over 130 organizations, including 114 US-based firms, were compromised in a massive phishing campaign that spoofed multi-factor authentication systems and obtained Okta identity credentials and codes through targeted text messages. The attack, dubbed "0ktapus," is believed to have originated from targeting telecommunications companies to obtain phone numbers, with the full extent of the campaign remaining unknown.

NSO Group Hacking WhatsApp Despite Court Order

Severity: 8/10

WhatsApp has caught the NSO Group phishing its users, in violation of a court order. The NSO Group is known for its spyware, and this incident highlights its disregard for cybersecurity and respect for court orders meant to safeguard users. This development demonstrates a significant breach of trust and further highlights cybersecurity concerns.
