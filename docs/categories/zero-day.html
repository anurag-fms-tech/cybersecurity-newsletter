Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9/10

A massive phishing campaign, dubbed "0ktapus," has compromised 9,931 accounts across over 130 organizations. The campaign spoofed a multi-factor authentication system, targeting companies including 114 US-based firms and those in 68 other countries. Threat actors aimed to obtain Okta identity credentials and MFA codes, often beginning by targeting telecommunications companies to access phone numbers used in attacks.
