Hackers Used Meta’s AI Support Bot to Seize Instagram Accounts

Severity: 8/10

The Instagram accounts for the Obama White House and the U.S. Space Force's Chief Master Sergeant were briefly defaced with pro-Iranian content after hackers used a vulnerability in Meta's AI support assistant bot to reset passwords. The exploit involved tricking the bot into linking an email address to an account, sending a one-time code for a password reset. Enabling multi-factor authentication (MFA) blocked the attack.
