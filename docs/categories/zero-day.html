Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9 (Note: '0ktapus' is a known threat group involved in phishing attacks that result in significant data breaches, indicating a high cybersecurity severity level)/10

A massive phishing campaign compromised over 9,931 accounts across 130 organizations worldwide. The attacks, tied to the "0ktapus" group, targeted telecommunications companies to obtain phone numbers, then sent fake Okta authentication links via text messages, tricking employees into revealing multi-factor authentication codes, compromising identities and access management credentials at 114 US-based companies and 68 international organizations.
