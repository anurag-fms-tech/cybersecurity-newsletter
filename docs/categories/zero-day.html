Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

Over 130 companies were targeted in a massive phishing campaign that compromised 9,931 accounts. The campaign, dubbed "0ktapus," spoofed Okta's multi-factor authentication system, tricking employees into providing login credentials and MFA codes via fake Okta pages. The attack affected 114 US firms and organizations in 68 countries, with the true extent of the damage still unknown.
