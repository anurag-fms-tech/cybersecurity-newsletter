Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 7/10

A massive phishing campaign compromised over 9,931 accounts at 130 organizations, with 114 US-based firms and 68 other countries affected. The "0ktapus" attackers targeted employees of Twilio, Cloudflare, and other companies by spoofing Okta's multi-factor authentication system, obtaining victims' Okta identity credentials and MFA codes through text messages with phishing links to mimic Okta's login page.
