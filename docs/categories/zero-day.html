AIs Compress Exploit Timeline

Severity: 8/10

A rumor of a vulnerability is now enough for AI agents to locate and weaponize the flaw, allowing attackers—and even the reporter’s own bots—to craft exploits before a public patch appears. This rapid discovery outpaces traditional open‑source embargoes, prompting experts like Simon Willison to call for new security‑response processes to protect open‑source communities.
