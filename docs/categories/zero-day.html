Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8 (This rating may vary depending on context and new information. The rating of 8 suggests a significant and widespread cyberattack, potentially leading to large-scale data breaches and financial losses.)/10

Over 130 companies were targeted in a massive phishing campaign that spoofed a multi-factor authentication system. Threat actors obtained Okta identity credentials and MFA codes from users, sending them text messages with phishing links mimicking Okta authentication pages. The campaign, dubbed "0ktapus," impacted 114 US-based firms and 68 additional countries, with the full scope still unknown.
