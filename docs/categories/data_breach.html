A leaked GitHub token led to a massive breach at Danish pharmaceutical giant Novo Nordisk, where attackers accessed internal systems, pseudonymized patient data, and sensitive company information, including source code and proprietary drug research. Salesforce Data Thefts Continue via Klue App Compromise

Severity: 8/10

Klue's Battlecards app has been compromised, allowing threat actors to steal Salesforce customer data. The attack resembles previous breaches involving third-party app integrations, such as Salesloft Drift and Gainsight. Threat actors used OAuth tokens to access and exfiltrate data via the Salesforce REST API, with one attack conducting nearly 1000 queries in 15 minutes.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

Over 2.5 million loan recipients from EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority (OSLA) may face future trouble after their personal data was exposed in a breach. The breach occurred between June 1, 2022 and July 22, 2022, with vulnerable information including names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, and social security numbers. The breach was discovered on August 17, 2022.

Cybercriminals Are Selling Access to Chinese Surveillance Cameras

Severity: 6/10

Over 80,000 Hikvision surveillance cameras worldwide remain unfixed after an 11-month-old critical CVE vulnerability was discovered. The flaw, with a 9.8 out of 10 rating, can be exploited for command injection, leaving thousands of organizations exposed. Hackers have been seeking to exploit the vulnerability on dark web forums and potential threat groups such as Chinese hackers and Russian actors are speculated to be behind the potential damage.

ICO Cautions Healthcare Worker After Princess of Wales Incident

Severity: 1/10

The UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) has decided not to pursue a criminal investigation against a former nurse who tried to access and sell Princess Kate's medical records after an abdominal surgery in 2023. Instead, they issued a formal caution for breaching the Data Protection Act, stating it was a "proportionate enforcement response" due to the "clear breach of trust."
