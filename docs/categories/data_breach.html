Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

A breach of Nelnet Servicing, the platform used by EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2.5 million student‑loan borrowers. Discovered on August 17 2022, the intrusion—likely stemming from a July 21 vulnerability—allowed unauthorized access to names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers, though financial details remained safe. Both lenders are notifying affected borrowers.
