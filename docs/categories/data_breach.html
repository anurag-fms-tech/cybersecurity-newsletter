Dutch Police Arrest ‘Reformed’ Hacker in Shiny Hunters Investigation

Severity: 2/10

Dutch authorities arrested 24‑year‑old Pepijn van der Stap, a convicted hacker known as “Umbreon,” for allegedly aiding ShinyHunters’ recent data thefts and extortions, including stealing FBI data and blackmailing ransomware gang Cl0p. Van der Stap, previously sentenced to four years for selling millions of Dutch records, had been working as an offensive security lead before his September 2026 detention.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority disclosed that a breach of Nelnet Servicing, their loan‑servicing platform, exposed personal data of 2.5 million borrowers. The intrusion, discovered Aug. 17 2022, accessed names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers, but not financial details. Nelnet’s cybersecurity team sealed the system and hired forensic experts; the breach occurred between June 1 and July 22, 2022.
