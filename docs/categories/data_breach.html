Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8 This is because a large-scale breach with over 2.5 million records exposed potentially puts many individuals at risk of identity theft and financial loss, making it a serious cybersecurity incident./10

Over 2.5 million people, including student loan account holders, had their personal data exposed in a data breach at Nelnet Servicing. The breach, which occurred between June 1 and July 22, 2022, accessed users' names, addresses, email, phone numbers, and social security numbers but did not expose financial information. The breach was discovered on August 17, 2022 and is being investigated with forensic experts.

Watering Hole Attacks Push ScanBox Keylogger

Severity: 7/10

Researchers discovered a watering hole attack by APT TA423 (Red Ladon), a China-based group, targeting Australian organizations and South China Sea-based energy firms. The attack used targeted messages from Australian news websites to distribute the ScanBox recon tool via JavaScript, potentially stealing information without malware installation from mid-April to mid-June 2022.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 9 (Note: A 9 is given due to the significant number of firms affected and the '0ktapus' threat group, likely being a sophisticated or targeted group)/10

Researchers discovered a massive phishing campaign that compromised 9,931 accounts across 130 organizations worldwide, using fake Okta login pages to steal MFA codes and identity credentials. The campaign, dubbed "0ktapus" by researchers, primarily targeted US-based companies and telecom operators, with 114 US firms and 68 additional international victims affected.

ICE Is Buying Access to Credit Card Records

Severity: 9/10

US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is purchasing information from data brokers to gather intelligence on individuals who obtained credit cards, compromising their privacy through this process. This has sparked calls for stronger data protection legislation, such as The Fourth Amendment Is Not For Sale Act, to safeguard American's private information.
