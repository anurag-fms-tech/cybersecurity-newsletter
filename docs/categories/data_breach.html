Market Research Giant Ipsos Investigating Breach of Internal System

Severity: 7/10

French market‑research firm Ipsos disclosed a breach of an internal system on Nov 16, five days after reporting it to authorities. The intrusion has not disrupted operations, and no data theft has been confirmed. Ipsos engaged external security experts, launched its response plan, and is investigating the incident’s scope. The company will notify affected clients if any sensitive information is found to be compromised.

Fandango Warns Ticket Buyers of Payment Card Data Exposure

Severity: 8/10

Fandango disclosed that an unauthorized party accessed payment‑card data—card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, billing ZIP codes—and email addresses from transactions made on its checkout page between Oct. 8‑11, 2026. The breach’s cause hasn’t been revealed, but the flaw is fixed and security tightened. No names, SSNs or IDs were exposed; fraud isn’t yet known. Affected customers (913 in Oregon, 18 in Vermont) receive two years of free credit‑monitoring, dark‑web scanning and identity‑theft protection.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

A breach at Nelnet Servicing, the platform used by EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2.5 million student‑loan borrowers. Between June 1 and July 22 2022, unauthorized actors accessed names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers (financial details were not compromised). The intrusion was discovered on August 17, after Nelnet reported a vulnerability on July 21, prompting immediate security actions and a forensic investigation.
