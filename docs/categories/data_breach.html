State Bank of India Suffers a Customer PII Data Leak

Severity: 9/10

An anonymous hacker leaked a batch of State Bank of India customer data, prompting the bank to acknowledge 3,897 records exposed online. While SBI downplayed the breach as affecting “a very, very small number” of customers, experts warn the leak could have serious fallout because India’s underground market trades personal data aggressively. BreachForums user “Siriusim” posted a larger sample—375,000 rows and 2,722 PDFs—confirming the PII’s authenticity and highlighting the risk to affected individuals.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 7/10

A breach at Nelnet Servicing, the platform used by EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2.5 million student‑loan borrowers. Between June 1 and July 22, 2022, unauthorized parties accessed names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers, though financial details remained safe. The intrusion was discovered on August 17, 2022 and prompted a forensic investigation and system lockdown.
