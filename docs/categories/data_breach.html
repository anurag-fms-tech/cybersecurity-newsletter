Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

A breach at Nelnet Servicing, the platform used by EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2,501,324 student‑loan borrowers. Discovered on July 21 2022 and confirmed on August 17, attackers accessed names, home addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers, though financial details remained safe. Nelnet’s cybersecurity team secured the system, blocked the activity and engaged third‑party forensic investigators.

LaLiga Says Football Fans’ Personal Data Stolen

Severity: 6/10

Spain’s top football league, LaLiga, disclosed a data breach that exposed fans’ personally identifiable information on a fan‑services platform between Nov 14‑16. A cyber‑criminal accessed and exfiltrated registration data for users of LaLiga and club digital services; the intrusion was stopped on Nov 16, with no other systems affected. Authorities, including Spain’s data‑protection agency and cybercrime police, have been notified, and monitoring has been heightened. Experts warn that the sports sector historically under‑invests in security, urging clubs to adopt stricter safeguards as attackers may now use the stolen data for targeted scams.
