Severity: 9/10

Almost 2.5 million people were affected in a data breach at Nelnet Servicing, which exposed names, home addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, and social security numbers of student loan account holders. The breach occurred between June 1 and July 22, 2022, and was discovered on August 17, 2022, with users' financial information remaining secure.
