Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

Over 2.5 million people who have student loans with EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority (OSLA) may be at risk due to a data breach that exposed their personal information. The breach, which occurred in June or July 2022, included sensitive data such as names, addresses, and social security numbers but not financial info, discovered by Nelnet Servicing on August 17, 2022.

Cybercriminals Are Selling Access to Chinese Surveillance Cameras

Severity: 8/10

Tens of thousands of Hikvision surveillance cameras are vulnerable to an 11-month-old command injection flaw, with over 80,000 unpatched devices worldwide. The flaw, labeled critical by NIST, has been found in Russian dark web forums where leaked credentials are being sold, and experts speculate that Chinese and Russian threat groups may exploit it.
