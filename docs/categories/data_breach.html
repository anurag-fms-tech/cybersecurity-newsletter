Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 7/10

A breach at Nelnet Servicing, the platform used by EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2.5 million student‑loan borrowers. The incident, discovered on August 17 2022, involved unauthorized access to names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers (financial details were not compromised). The breach likely stemmed from a vulnerability identified on July 21 2022 and affected loans administered by both EdFinancial and OSLA.
