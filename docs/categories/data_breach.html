Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

Over 2.5 million people had their personal data exposed in a data breach involving Nelnet Servicing, a company that provides servicing systems for EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority. The breach occurred between June 1, 2022, and July 22, 2022, and exposed names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, and social security numbers, but not financial information.

Tentacles of ‘0ktapus’ Threat Group Victimize 130 Firms

Severity: 8/10

Over 130 companies worldwide, including 114 US-based firms, were impacted by a massive phishing campaign tied to identity and access management firm Okta. The campaign, dubbed 0ktapus, targeted employees with fake text messages mimicking Okta authentication pages, resulting in 9,931 compromised accounts. The attackers likely obtained phone numbers from initial targets in the telecom industry and used them to gain multi-factor authentication codes.

Cybercriminals Are Selling Access to Chinese Surveillance Cameras

Severity: 8/10

Over 80,000 Hikvision surveillance cameras remain unpatched for a critical 11-month-old command injection flaw (CVE-2021-36260), leaving thousands of organizations vulnerable. The exploit has a "critical" 9.8 out of 10 rating, and attackers have been seen selling leaked credentials on Russian dark web forums. Many users may not even be aware of the vulnerability due to the lack of automatic updates and unclear security indicators in IoT devices.
