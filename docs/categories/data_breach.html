Hex Package Manager Warns of Potential Data Breach

Severity: 7/10

Hex’s package manager for Elixir/Erlang (hex.pm) was breached on Nov 23 when attackers exploited an unauthenticated deserialization bug (CVE‑2026‑129514, CVSS 10) to run OS commands on six web servers. The intrusion was halted within minutes and the flaw patched, but reads of private data (emails, password hashes, API keys, 2FA secrets) may have occurred, and such accesses aren’t logged. Hex forced credential rotations, logged everyone out, and urges users to reset passwords, re‑enable 2FA, and avoid third‑party mirrors. Related libraries (Ash Framework, db_connection, flop) were also patched.

SAP Confirms Cyberattack, Source Code Reportedly Compromised

Severity: 9/10

Enterprise software giant SAP confirmed a cybersecurity incident after hackers obtained employee authentication tokens and accessed its GitHub repositories, copying and modifying source code. SAP says no customer data was compromised, and it has replaced signing keys, restricted GitHub access, and paused automated development. The breach’s scope, including any proprietary code exposure, remains unclear, and AI assistance is unverified.

FIFA Says It Has Been Victim of a Cyberattack Accessing ‘Personal and Business Information’

Severity: 8/10

FIFA said it suffered a cyber‑attack that accessed business data, but gave no details on timing or the exact information. The Handsome Boy Hacking Club posted screenshots and claimed over 12 GB of internal files were leaked. FIFA condemned the breach, is working with law‑enforcement, and notes it comes amid scrutiny of President Gianni Infantino’s World Cup stake‑sale plans and his bid for another term.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

A breach at Nelnet Servicing, the loan‑servicing platform for EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2,501,324 student‑loan borrowers. Between June 1 and July 22 2022, an unauthorized party accessed names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers, though financial details remained safe. The intrusion was discovered on August 17 2022; Nelnet’s cybersecurity team secured the system and engaged forensic investigators.
