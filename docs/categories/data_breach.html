Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

A breach at Nelnet Servicing, the platform used by EdFinancial and the Oklahoma Student Loan Authority, exposed personal data of 2.5 million student‑loan borrowers. Between June 1 and July 22 2022, unauthorized actors accessed names, addresses, emails, phone numbers and Social Security numbers, though financial details remained safe. Nelnet detected the vulnerability on July 21, disclosed it on August 17, and engaged forensic experts to investigate.
