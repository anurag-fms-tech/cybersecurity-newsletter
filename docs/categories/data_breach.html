Scattered Spider Hackers Plead Guilty on Day 1 of Trial

Severity: 6/10

Two men, Owen Flowers, 18, and Thalha Jubair, 20, pleaded guilty in the UK for their roles in a cyberattack on Transport for London in August 2024. They were part of the Scattered Spider group, which conducted numerous attacks worldwide. Jubair is also wanted by US law enforcement agencies for his involvement in over 120 computer network intrusions and $115 million in ransom payments.

‘Popa’ Botnet Linked to Publicly-Traded Israeli Firm

Severity: 8/10

Researchers have linked the massive Android botnet "Popa" to NetNut, a "residential proxy" provider operated by Israeli firm Alarum Technologies. Popa malware forces millions of consumer TV boxes to relay internet traffic for advertising fraud, account takeovers, and data scraping, with a plugin component associated with the Vo1d botnet targeting unofficial Android-based TV boxes.

Hackers Used Meta’s AI Support Bot to Seize Instagram Accounts

Severity: 8/10

Instagram accounts for the Obama White House and the U.S. Space Force's Chief Master Sergeant were briefly defaced with pro-Iranian content over the weekend. Hackers tricked Meta's AI support assistant into resetting passwords using a simple exploit involving a VPN and a specific chat query. The vulnerability was patched, but experts warn that AI-powered support systems create new attack surfaces and may be vulnerable to similar tactics, emphasizing the importance of multi-factor authentication.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 8/10

A data breach at Nelnet Servicing exposed the personal data of approximately 2.5 million individuals, affecting student loan account holders. The breach occurred between June 1 and July 22, 2022, and was discovered on August 17, 2022. Exposed information included names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, and social security numbers, but financial information was not affected.

Cybercriminals Are Selling Access to Chinese Surveillance Cameras

Severity: 8/10

Over 80,000 Hikvision surveillance cameras remain vulnerable to a 11-month-old command injection flaw (CVE-2021-36260), leaving thousands of organizations exposed. Despite a critical rating, the devices have failed to be patched, and researchers have found instances of hackers selling leaked credentials on dark web forums, suggesting potential exploitation by Chinese and Russian threat groups.

One Million Passports Leaked Online

Severity: 7/10

A database of almost a million passports worldwide was leaked online in a low-value ID verification system for cannabis dispensaries. This compromised a high-value credential, putting the passports at risk. The leak is suspected to be 2 months old, with no reported notifications of affected individuals as of June 26, 2026.

CMC Releases Analysis and Guidance for Education Sector After Canvas Data Breach

Severity: 6 This rating is based on the context that CMC (Center for Internet Security's Managed Security Service, also known as CMC - not to be confused with a construction company) has performed an analysis and provided guidance for handling a data breach in the education sector. While a data breach is a significant concern, the fact that a reputable organization is working to mitigate its impact suggests that the situation is being managed./10

The UK's Cyber Monitoring Centre (CMC) analyzed the Canvas cyber incident affecting Instructure's Learning Management System. Approximately 160 UK higher education institutions were affected, with threat actors exfiltrating confidential data. The incident is not considered a Category 1 event due to lower-than-threshold financial impact, but highlights differing financial profiles between data breach and disruption events.
