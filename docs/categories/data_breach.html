Big Brand Jobs Scam Targets Marketing Pros' Google Accounts

Severity: 8/10

A massive job-recruiting-focused phishing campaign is circulating, using legitimate platforms and disguising itself as prominent brands like Coca-Cola and Netflix to steal Google credentials from marketing professionals. The campaign uses "nested redirects" to evade detection, making it harder for users to recognize the phishing links.

Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 7/10

About 2.5 million people, including student loan account holders, had their personal data exposed in a data breach affecting Nelnet Servicing, which services loan authorities EdFinancial and Oklahoma Student Loan. The breach occurred between June 1 and July 22, 2022, compromising names, addresses, phone numbers, and social security numbers but not financial information.

Google Is Suing Chinese Scammers Who Are Using Gemini

Severity: 3/10

Google worked with carriers to block malicious texts from the phishing-as-a-service group Outsider Enterprise, which operated through Telegram. The group provided scam templates to non-technical individuals. Google's on-device scam detection in Google Messages likely caught some of these attempts, stopping around 10 billion scam texts every month.
