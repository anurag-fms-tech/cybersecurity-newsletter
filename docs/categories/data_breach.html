Student Loan Breach Exposes 2.5M Records

Severity: 7/10

A data breach affecting over 2.5 million student loan account holders occurred between June 1st and July 22nd, 2022, when Nelnet Servicing's system was accessed by an unauthorized party. The exposed information included names, addresses, email, phone numbers, and social security numbers, but users' financial information remained secure. The breach was not discovered until August 17th, 2022.

Cybercriminals Are Selling Access to Chinese Surveillance Cameras

Severity: 9/10

Tens of thousands of Hikvision surveillance cameras remain vulnerable to an 11-month-old command injection flaw (CVE-2021-36260) with over 80,000 devices unpatched worldwide. Research found multiple hacker attempts to exploit the flaw, potentially threatening Chinese and Russian state interests. Difficulty in securing IoT devices, lack of automatic updates, and poor user notification contribute to the ongoing problem.

On Flock License Plate Tracking Cameras

Severity: 4 (This news likely refers to private license plate tracking being used to monitor and track individuals, but the severity of this news can vary greatly depending on the extent and purpose of the tracking.)/10

A man was mistakenly tracked and arrested by Flock cameras using a partially entered license plate. The company's AI initially flagged his car as stolen due to a similar plate structure, and four others in Minnesota were also tracked. Flock initially defended their system, but the issue was resolved after the LAPD corrected their report. The incident highlights the potential for AI-powered surveillance to incorrectly identify and flag vehicles.
