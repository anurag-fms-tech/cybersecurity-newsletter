AI Use by the US Government

Severity: 2 This news is relatively low in terms of cybersecurity severity as it does not directly involve a known vulnerability, breach, or potential threat to systems or data. However, it could potentially lead to increased use of AI in government operations which may introduce new risks or vulnerabilities that need to be addressed, such as algorithmic bias and potential misuse of AI./10

The US federal government has quietly acknowledged 3,611 active or planned AI use cases, a 70% increase from 2020. Examples include AI-powered surveillance of grant applications, AI-driven inmate risk assessments, and AI-controlled nuclear reactors. The use of AI in these sensitive areas has raised concerns over individual freedom, public health, and safety.
