U.K. Sanctions Cryptomus for RU Money Laundering

Severity: 3/10

The United Kingdom imposed sanctions on Cryptomus (formerly Certa Payments/Xeltox Enterprises), a cryptocurrency platform that has become a key conduit for ransomware, drug, darknet and other cyber‑crime payments aiding Russia’s evasion of financial restrictions. Assets in U.K. banks are frozen and British firms barred from dealing with Cryptomus, Heleket, VexPay, TokenSpot and Tsunami Payments. Chainalysis and TRM Labs cite billions in illicit transactions processed by the platform.

PapRAT Cyber-Espionage Campaign Leaves a 'Bitter' Taste in South Asia

Severity: 7/10

FortiGuard Labs reports that the India‑linked cyber‑espionage group Bitter (TA397) is intensifying attacks on regional organizations. Using three distinct infection chains, the group exploits native Windows functions and a revamped PapRAT remote‑access Trojan, now rewritten in Rust with expanded commands, PowerShell execution, sleep and clean‑exit features. Targets include government, energy and engineering firms, aiming for persistent control.

Studying Fraudulent North Korean IT Workers

Severity: 4/10

Researchers created a counterfeit startup to lure and analyze fraudulent North Korean IT workers posing as legitimate hires. Their findings reveal that once hired, these operatives gain authorized network access, turning HR processes into an attack surface and posing a classic insider‑threat risk. Past cases show North Korea exploits stolen credentials for espionage, underscoring the need for rigorous vetting and rapid credential resets if a spy is discovered.
