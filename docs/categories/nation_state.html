Trump, Tech Giants Strike Voluntary AI Safety Accord

Severity: 2/10

The White House’s “Accord on Super Intelligence,” signed Sept. 30, 2026, sees six AI leaders—OpenAI, Google, Meta, Nvidia, xAI and Anthropic—voluntarily adopt tighter safety measures. Companies must install internal controls, create dedicated safety teams, undergo independent audits, and give boards greater oversight of AI risks such as cyber‑, bio‑ and chemical threats. President Trump opposes mandatory guardrails, but the accord hints future regulation may follow.

South Africa Seeks Help After Cyberattack Targets Air Traffic Control

Severity: 9/10

South African state‑owned Air Traffic and Navigation Services (ATNS), which controls about 10 % of global airspace, detected ransomware‑linked malware in an operational‑technology network used for weather and air‑traffic services. The technical team halted the attack, but ATNS has issued an RFQ for cyber‑forensics to probe the breach and a possible insider data theft, noting clues of data exfiltration to China. Ransomware assaults on aviation rose sixfold in 2025, with 27 major incidents reported in the preceding 16 months.
