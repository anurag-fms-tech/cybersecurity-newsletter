France Is Blaming Iran for the Fridge Cyberattack

Severity: 8/10

U.S. intelligence agencies have issued a low‑confidence assessment that Iran, not a specific unit like the Revolutionary Guards, was behind recent cyber‑attacks on French infrastructure, including IoT systems. Officials say the judgment is based on fragmented intel and could shift as more data emerges. Iran is also suspected of similar attacks elsewhere in Europe, though investigations are nascent.
