Russian APT 'Gamaredon' Upgrades Its Arsenal, Requiring New Defenses

Severity: 7/10

A Russian cyber espionage group, Gamaredon, has upgraded its tactics, allowing it to launch more successful attacks. It has developed new tools and techniques, including a malware that checks for USB drives and smuggles malware onto them. To counter this, organizations should implement fresh strategies and consider scanning USB files, sanitizing them, or banning unvetted drives.

Local Police Collusion Hampers Crackdown on Asian Scam Centers

Severity: 8 This rating is based on the information not directly suggesting a major hack or security breach but rather a non-technical factor (police collusion) affecting law enforcement in the fight against cybercrime./10

Tens of billions of dollars from cybercrime fuel scam centers in Asia and the South Pacific, with local corruption hindering law enforcement efforts. Despite high-profile arrests, scams continue to dominate crime in the region, with estimated annual revenue of $40 billion from romance fraud and investment scams, operated by some with ties to human trafficking.
