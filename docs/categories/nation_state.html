President Trump Taps Kirsten Davies as National Cyber Director

Severity: 1/10

Kirsten Davies, former CIO of the Department of War and veteran CISO/CSO at Unilever, HP, Siemens and Barclays, has been named the new U.S. National Cyber Director, succeeding Sean Cairncross. Her appointment comes as the U.S. grapples with widespread outages—Microsoft 365, FAA communications, JPMorgan, United Airlines and NSA’s NSANet—and a surge in AI‑driven attacks using China’s Artex framework. The administration says the incidents stem from patching and system upgrades, not coordinated state‑sponsored attacks.

France Is Blaming Iran for the Fridge Cyberattack

Severity: 8/10

U.S. intelligence agencies have issued a “low‑confidence” assessment that Iran, rather than a specific unit such as the Revolutionary Guard, was behind recent cyber‑attacks targeting French infrastructure. The judgment is based on fragmented intelligence and could change as more data emerge. Officials also think Iran is probably linked to similar attacks across Europe, though those probes are still in early stages.
