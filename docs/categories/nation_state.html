AI Use by the US Government

Severity: 5 (The score is relatively low since the news does not directly indicate any security issue or breach, but rather an adoption or development of AI technology by the government which inherently could have some associated cybersecurity risks.)/10

The US federal government has quietly acknowledged 3,611 active or planned AI use cases, a 70% increase since the previous administration. These include AI-powered plans to scan grant applications and flag ideologically unaligned submissions, assess inmate misconduct risk, and control nuclear reactors. Concerns have been raised about AI overstepping human decision-making in life-and-death situations, but proponents argue these programs could be implemented responsibly with proper validation and bipartisan agreement.
