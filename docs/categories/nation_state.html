How Candidates Could Use AI for Good

Severity: 1/10

AI is deepening U.S. midterm anxieties: politicians deploy deep‑fake lies while the White House spreads “slopaganda.” Yet AI could improve campaigning by enabling one‑to‑one and many‑to‑many voter engagement, gathering rich public input beyond simple surveys. Japan’s Team Mirai party uses an AI interview bot to collect 300 k messages, influencing policy and winning 12 Diet seats. The piece urges U.S. candidates to adopt similar democratic AI tools.
