Russia's Hybrid Cyber-Physical War in Europe Heats Up

Severity: 9/10

Recorded Future reports that Russia is hybrid warfare against European nations supporting Ukraine. Tactics include cyber sabotage of water, wastewater, and energy systems in Norway, Poland, and Germany; AI‑generated disinformation campaigns impersonating media in France and Norway; and drone incursions near Estonia, Romania’s offshore gas field, and a German airport. Risk to firms rises if they aid Ukraine’s war effort.

On Anthropic’s AI Misuse Report

Severity: 6/10

Anthropic released a comprehensive report on misuse of its Claude AI model, cataloguing every detected abuse case. Analyst Daniel Meissler distilled the findings into 117 distinct instances, highlighting patterns of malicious prompting, privacy violations, and attempts to subvert the system’s safeguards. The summary underscores growing concerns over AI exploitation and the need for stronger governance and detection mechanisms.

CISA Unveils Election Security Plan Ahead of 2026 Midterms

Severity: 2/10

CISA issued an Election Infrastructure Security Plan for the November 2026 midterms, advising state, local, and federal officials on protecting physical assets—polling sites, storage facilities, tabulation centers—and ICT systems like voter‑registration databases, voting machines, and result reporting. The agency warns threat actors will target these systems and stresses partnerships, defenses, and threat intelligence. After 2025 funding cuts that weakened EI‑ISAC, lawmakers have urged its restoration.
