25 Years of Mass Surveillance Is Enough

Severity: 2/10

The post‑9/11 shift from targeted to mass surveillance—initially justified by counter‑terrorism—has become a routine tool for law enforcement, ICE, and private security, relying heavily on data harvested by tech firms and brokers. Companies like Google, Facebook, and license‑plate‑reader vendors feed massive datasets to agencies, which now buy information outright. With AI enhancing analysis, the article calls for a thorough cost‑benefit review of this pervasive surveillance model and its impact on civil liberties.
