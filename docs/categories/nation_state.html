From Bobmojis to Bobbleheads: How the Democratic Party Built a Security-First Culture

Severity: 5/10

Two former chief security officers of the Democratic National Committee, Bob Lord and Steve Tran, emphasized the need for a "security-first mindset" at Black Hat USA 2026. They shared strategies such as introducing "absurd" reminders, like security-themed bathroom stickers and a "Security Feud" game, to instill security habits in employees. Their approaches show that creative measures can help build a strong security culture in various organizations.
