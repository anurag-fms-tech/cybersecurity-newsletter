Leaked Russian Cyber-Operations Training Materials

Severity: 7/10

New research reveals a formal Russian pipeline that channels university graduates into cyber‑military roles. The Bauman study details a “force‑generation” system feeding the GRU, Main Operational Directorate, and the 8th Directorate (communications, cryptography, info‑security). It links 2024 graduate Aleksei Kondrashov to Military Unit 74455 (Sandworm), famed for NotPetya and other Ukraine attacks, but notes individual participation isn’t proven. The findings portray Russia’s cyber capability as an institutional apparatus rather than isolated hacker groups.

A series of essays by Nathan E. Sanders and a co‑author for *The Renovator* examines real‑world democratic technologies.
