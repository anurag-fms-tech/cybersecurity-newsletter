How Technology Empowers—and Imperils—Dictators

Severity: 6/10

Russia and China are deploying AI to tighten authoritarian control, replacing human censors, propagandists and officials with synthetic anchors, facial‑recognition surveillance, and algorithmic performance reviews. UK and Allies Warn of Cyber Threat from China’s Integrity Technology Group

Severity: 8/10

The US, UK and allied nations issued a joint alert on October 8 targeting Integrity Technology Group, a sanctioned Chinese firm that backs prolific threat actors such as Flax Typhoon (Ethereal Panda, Red Juliett). The advisory details the group’s TTPs—building and selling cyber tools, hosting infrastructure, and compromising global networks—to fuel China’s cyber‑espionage ecosystem. It includes extensive IoCs, mitigation steps, and urges security teams to treat the threat seriously.
