FBI Publishes First-Ever Cyber Strategy, With Focus on Disrupting Threat Actors

Severity: 6/10

The FBI released its first Cyber Strategy, outlining a proactive, disruption‑focused approach to counter financially‑motivated cybercriminals and state‑sponsored actors operating abroad. The plan emphasizes rapid victim support, integrated partnerships, and continuous investment in tools to take down infrastructure, seize assets, and expose adversaries before they act. Experts note the shift from long‑term prosecutions to immediate operational wins.
