A Video Screen That Is Also a Camera

Severity: 1/10

Researchers from ETH Zurich created a "Fourier pixel" that can simultaneously generate and sense arbitrary light fields, manipulating light's intensity, oscillation phases, and polarization. This development brings them closer to creating a telescreen-like device that can transmit and receive information simultaneously, with the potential to be used in advanced cameras and video technology.
