U.K. Sanctions Cryptomus for RU Money Laundering

Severity: 6/10

UK sanctions target Cryptomus (now Heleket), a platform used for ransomware, drug, darknet and illicit payments that help Russia evade restrictions. Measures freeze UK assets and bar firms from dealing with Cryptomus, VexPay, TokenSpot and Tsunami Payments. Chainalysis and TRM Labs say service processed over $7 billion since 2022, including funds for child‑abuse, terrorism and human trafficking, despite Canadian fines and KYC attempts.

UK Cyber Chief Says the UK Will Face AI Cyberattacks on Critical Infrastructure by 2028

Severity: 8/10

Britain’s cyber chief warns that by 2028 AI‑driven attacks will target the UK’s critical infrastructure, with state actors such as China and Russia already using the technology to amplify existing tactics. AI‑found flaws in a core web‑language and recent ransomware hits on utilities show the growing risk. The NCSC says cyber‑crime costs nearly $20 billion a year and urges organisations to update software, restrict admin access and back up data.

Studying Fraudulent North Korean IT Workers

Severity: 3/10

Researchers created a fictitious startup to attract and study fraudulent North Korean IT workers, exposing how these operatives infiltrate companies through legitimate hiring processes. Once employed, they gain insider access without needing zero‑day exploits, using stolen employee credentials to spy, exfiltrate data, and maintain long‑term footholds. The experiment highlights HR as a critical attack surface and stresses rigorous vetting, login rotation, and detection methods.

Russia-Aligned Gorbag Targets Ukraine for Intelligence

Severity: 8/10

A Russia‑aligned cyberespionage group dubbed “Gorbag” has been targeting Ukrainian justice, law‑enforcement, local government and defense entities since at least February 2025. Using a custom infostealer called BeautyStealer, it harvests credentials, VPN configs, cookies, browser data and sensitive documents, including e‑signature keys. The campaign began with spear‑phishing Excel macros and later shifted to malicious RAR archives exploiting CVE‑2025‑8088, reflecting Russia’s wartime intelligence priorities.
