President Trump Taps Kirsten Davies as National Cyber Director

Severity: 1/10

Kirsten Davies, former DOW CIO and veteran CISO at Unilever, HP, Siemens and others, has been named the new U.S. National Cyber Director, tasked with advancing President Trump’s “America First” cyber agenda. Her appointment comes as the nation grapples with high‑profile outages—from Microsoft 365 and FAA communications to JPMorgan and United Airlines—and a rise in AI‑driven attacks linked to China’s Artex framework. The administration says the disruptions are unrelated system‑maintenance issues, not coordinated cyber‑attacks.

France Is Blaming Iran for the Fridge Cyberattack

Severity: 5/10

U.S. intelligence agencies have issued a “low‑confidence” judgment that Iran, rather than a specific unit like the Revolutionary Guard, was behind recent cyber‑attacks on French infrastructure and likely responsible for similar incidents across Europe. The assessment, based on fragmented intelligence, could change as investigations progress.

Suspected North Korea-Aligned Group Hides C2 on Solana Blockchain

Severity: 9/10

A financially motivated threat group, tracked as UAT‑12643 and suspected of North Korean APT links, hid its C2 server addresses on the Solana blockchain. Using a Go‑based Overlord RAT, an infostealer and a crypto‑clipboard hijacker, the malware fetched encrypted C2 data from wallet memos, resisted removal (triggering BSODs and persisting in Windows Recovery), and performed keylogging, screen capture, file control, lateral movement and in‑memory plugin delivery. Targets included crypto holders, gamers and VoIP users.
