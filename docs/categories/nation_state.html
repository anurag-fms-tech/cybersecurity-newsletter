Pentagon Renames CDAO ‘Super Intelligence Office’

Severity: 1/10

The Pentagon has renamed its Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office to the “Super Intelligence Office,” complying with President Trump’s executive order that mandates federal agencies use the term “Super Intelligence” instead of AI. Led by Chief Super Intelligence Officer Cameron Stanley, the office moved its resources to the SI.mil domain and will continue programs such as the Maven Smart System and War Data Platform. The rebrand signals the DoD’s shift from experimental AI projects to full‑scale deployment of sovereign “Super Intelligence” for military superiority, mirroring similar moves by the Justice Department and a new government‑wide Super Intelligence Force.
