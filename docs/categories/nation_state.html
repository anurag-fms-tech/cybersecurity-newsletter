Leaked Russian Cyber-Operations Training Materials

Severity: 8/10

New documents reveal a formal “force‑generation” pipeline feeding Russia’s General Staff cyber components—including the GRU, Main Operational Directorate and 8th Directorate responsible for protected communications, cryptography and information security. They link 2024 Bauman University graduate Aleksei Kondrashov to Military Unit 74455 (Sandworm), famed for NotPetya and other attacks. While individual participation isn’t proven, the material shows Moscow has institutionalized a university‑to‑military route for training cyber‑intelligence personnel.
