European Commission Confirms AI-Assisted Cyberattack Disrupted Services

Severity: 9/10

The European Commission confirmed an AI‑assisted cyberattack that struck its IT systems in late October, forcing temporary shutdown of several applications—including HR, medical‑reimbursement, and translation platforms. Detected on Oct 22 and confirmed on Oct 26, the breach appears contained with no evidence of staff data loss, and no ransom demand was reported. EU security agency CERT‑EU linked the attack to the publicly available AI penetration‑testing framework Artex, warning that such tools could enable faster, more automated assaults.

France Is Blaming Iran for the Fridge Cyberattack

Severity: 5/10

U.S. intelligence agencies have issued a “low‑confidence” judgment that Iran, though not a specific unit like the Revolutionary Guards, likely orchestrated recent cyber‑attacks on French infrastructure and may be behind similar incidents across Europe. The assessment is based on fragmented intelligence and could change as further information emerges. This judgment reflects early‑stage investigations into the attacks.
