FBI Publishes First-Ever Cyber Strategy, With Focus on Disrupting Threat Actors

Severity: 2/10

The FBI released its first Cyber Strategy, outlining a proactive, disruption‑focused approach to combat financially‑motivated cybercriminals and state‑sponsored actors operating beyond U.S. jurisdiction. The plan emphasizes rapid victim support, integrated partnerships, and investment in tools to shut down infrastructure, seize funds, and expose tactics before attacks occur. Experts note this marks a shift from traditional prosecution‑centric enforcement toward broader, operational takedowns.
