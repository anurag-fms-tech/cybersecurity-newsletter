Why TLP should not replace your internal information classification, (Sat, Oct 10th)

Threat: Data Breach
Severity: 1/10

The Traffic Light Protocol (TLP 2.0) is a sharing‑control standard, not an internal classification system. Some firms are wrongly swapping their own data‑classification schemes for TLP labels, assuming the simple “red/amber/green/white” scale covers handling, encryption, storage and retention rules. Because TLP only dictates *who* may receive information—not *how* it must be protected—its labels can clash with internal policies and cause unintended broader distribution, especially when “TLP:GREEN” is misinterpreted as strictly internal.
