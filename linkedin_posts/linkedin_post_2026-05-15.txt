Cybersecurity Intelligence Brief

1. Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Controller Auth Bypass Actively Exploited to Gain Admin Access
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 9/10
Cisco has released updates to address a max-severity authentication bypass flaw in its Catalyst SD-WAN Controller, allowing an attacker to bypass authentication and obtain administrative privileges. The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-20182, has been exploited in limited attacks and carries a CVSS score of 10.0. Cisco urges customers to apply the latest updates as soon as possible, citing awareness of limited exploitation in May 2026.

2. Stealer Backdoor Found in 3 Node-IPC Versions Targeting Developer Secrets
Threat: Malware | Severity: 8

(Note: The term "stealer" typically refers to malware designed to steal sensitive information, such as developer secrets, and a backdoor is a method of unauthorized access to a computer system. Based on this information, the cybersecurity severity appears to be reasonably high.)/10
Cybersecurity researchers found three malicious versions of node-ipc on npm: 9.1.6, 9.2.3, and 12.0.1. The malware, hidden in the package, can harvest 90 types of developer and cloud credentials, compressing and exfiltrating them to a command-and-control server through DNS logic. The compromised package was added 21 months after the last update, with the malicious payload hidden in the code, performing a fingerprint check and system enumeration.

4. Ghostwriter Targets Ukrainian Government With Geofenced PDF Phishing, Cobalt Strike
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 8/10
Belarus-aligned threat group Ghostwriter, tracked under various monikers, has been linked to a fresh set of attacks targeting Ukrainian governmental organizations. Ghostwriter has been active since 2016 and has continuously updated its toolset to evade detection. Recent attacks used spear-phishing attachments with PDF decoy documents, often impersonating legitimate companies, to deploy JavaScript payloads and Cobalt Strike Beacon.

5. PraisonAI CVE-2026-44338 Auth Bypass Targeted Within Hours of Disclosure
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 7.8

Explanation: 

A CVE-2026-44338 is a specific, newly disclosed vulnerability. Auth bypass suggests that it's a serious issue because authenticated users should be able to access certain areas of the application securely. The targeted nature of the attack, happening within hours of disclosure, may indicate that it's a high-value vulnerability./10
Threat actors exploited a newly disclosed PraisonAI vulnerability, CVE-2026-44338, within four hours of its public disclosure. The flaw allows unauthenticated access to sensitive endpoints. PraisonAI has patched the issue in version 4.6.34. Security researcher Shmulik Cohen discovered the bug, and Sysdig observed attempts to exploit it within hours.
