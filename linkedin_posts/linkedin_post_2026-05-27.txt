1. MuddyWater Uses DLL Side-Loading in Espionage Campaign Targeting 9 Countries
Threat: Nation State | Severity: 8/10

4. MFA Prompt Bombing: Why Your Second Factor Isn't Saving You
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 8

This rating reflects the potential severity of the issue described in the news headline, as it implies a vulnerability in the multi-factor authentication (MFA) process that could compromise security./10
Attacks on push-based multi-factor authentication (MFA) techniques can compromise even strong security measures. Attackers trick users into approving login requests by repeatedly prompting or using vishing calls, then gain access. The 2022 Cisco breach is a notorious example, highlighting the danger of this method, which can evade security systems. Tools specifically designed to address this gap, like Specops Secure Access, may be necessary.

5. CERT-In Recommends 12-Hour Patching for Internet-Facing Flaws Amid AI-Assisted Attacks
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 8/10
The Indian CERT-In has issued guidelines requiring organizations to patch critical security vulnerabilities within 12 hours of being flagged to mitigate AI-assisted cyber threats. As threat actors increasingly rely on AI for attack preparation, CERT-In warns that exploitation timelines will collapse and attacks will become autonomous, necessitating continuous threat assessment and proactive measures.
