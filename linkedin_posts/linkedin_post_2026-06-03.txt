Cybersecurity Intelligence Brief

1. Google June 2026 Android Update Patches 124 Flaws, One Actively Exploited
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 10/10
Google patched 124 Android security flaws, including a high-severity "privilege escalation" bug (CVE-2025-48595) with a CVSS score of 8.4, which has been exploited. The vulnerability impacts Android versions 14-16 and may be under active exploitation, allowing code execution and local escalation of privilege without user interaction.

2. Gamaredon Exploits WinRAR to Deliver GammaWorm and GammaSteel Against Ukraine
Threat: Malware | Severity: 9/10
Russian hacking group Gamaredon is exploiting a WinRAR vulnerability (CVE-2025-8088) to deliver multiple malware families, including a VBScript worm (GammaWorm) and information stealer (GammaSteel). The malware uses legitimate platforms, NTFS Alternate Data Streams, and AWS S3 buckets to conceal its activities and enable long-term espionage operations, primarily targeting Ukrainian government, military, and critical infrastructure entities.

3. Oracle WebLogic CVE-2024-21182 Added to KEV Catalog After Active Exploitation
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 10/10
The US Cybersecurity agency (CISA) has added a high-severity security flaw, CVE-2024-21182, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, which impacts Oracle WebLogic Server. The vulnerability, patched in July 2024, allows an attacker with network access to take control of susceptible servers. FCEB agencies are recommended to apply fixes by June 4, 2026, to secure their networks.

4. AI-Driven Exploitation is Destroying Vulnerability Management. Here’s How to Handle It.
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 8

(This rating is based on the potential threat of AI-driven exploitation on vulnerability management, which can lead to widespread and sophisticated cyberattacks.)/10
Cybersecurity threats are evolving rapidly due to AI-driven exploitation. Vulnerabilities are being discovered, reproduced, and weaponized in hours, not days. While patching is essential, it's no longer enough to address the influx of disclosed vulnerabilities. Attackers use AI to identify vulnerabilities with speed, widening the  gap with defenders who operate on slower timelines, causing remediation and patching efforts to fall behind, making 'patch faster' a unrealistic solution.

5. How Leading Organizations Are Turning EDR Into Operational Resilience
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 3

This news appears to be more related to cybersecurity practices and strategies, rather than a specific security threat or breach./10
Many organizations invest in endpoint detection and response (EDR) for enhanced cybersecurity, but struggle to operationalize its capabilities due to overwhelmed teams, high alert volumes, and stretched response capacity. To stay ahead, effective detection and response require continuous monitoring, prioritization, and rapid containment, beyond just visibility, to proactively reduce attacker opportunity and achieve sustainable operational cyber resilience.
