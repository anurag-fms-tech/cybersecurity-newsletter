2. Attackers Target Unpatched CryptoPayment Gateway Flaw That Can Expose Wallet Credentials
Threat: Vulnerability | Severity: 8/10
Defused Cyber reports active exploitation of CVE‑2026‑81648, a critical (CVSS 10.0) flaw in the CryptoPayment Gateway WordPress plugin (versions 1.2.1‑1.2.2). The unpatched vulnerability lets unauthenticated users delete files, overwrite gateway settings, and retrieve stored wallet credentials. No fix exists; the plugin has been removed from the WordPress directory. Site owners should uninstall it, rotate wallet keys, and audit for suspicious changes.

4. Google Disrupts 1.3 Million-Device Android Botnet, Says TV Box Maker Sold Criminal Access
Threat: Malware | Severity: 9/10
Google helped dismantle a botnet of 1.3 million TV boxes that Videostrong, a maker, embedded with a backdoor (x.cpe) sold for ad fraud and traffic hijacking. It remains on devices and cannot be removed by users. Google sued to shut down C2 domains, cut ties with Videostrong and barred it from certifications. Partners included Japan’s NPA, Lumen, HUMAN Security and Plume.

5. DarkBlinders Compromises Israeli Target and Iraqi Kurdish Government Cloud Environment
Threat: Data Breach | Severity: 9/10
Iran‑aligned hackers, dubbed DarkBlinders, ran a cyber‑espionage campaign from Aug‑Oct 2026 against Israel and the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, compromising a high‑profile Israeli figure and a government cloud environment. Using spear‑phishing, they delivered trojanized utilities that installed the SHELBYLOADER/DLL‑sideloading backdoor SHELBYC2 with GitHub C2. Earlier “Blinder Tunnel” ops posed as Dubai Airports recruiting, sending fake coding challenges to embed the same loader and a tunneling tool called Blackwood.
